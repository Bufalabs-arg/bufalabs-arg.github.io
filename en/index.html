FRIDAY lives in your home.

A free AI assistant that listens, talks back and actually does things on your computer and your phone. Made in Argentina by BufaLabs.

Heads-up: for now FRIDAY speaks Spanish (Argentina). It runs on free AI services, so it costs nothing.

  • It sees your screen

    Ask “what is wrong here?” and it looks at what you have open and explains it. Only when you ask.

  • It researches for you

    “Research which laptop to buy”: it reads several pages, tells you how it is going and leaves you a full report.

  • It reads your files

    “What does the last PDF I downloaded say?” It finds it and sums it up. You can also drop a file on it.

  • It helps you study

    Study mode with timed sessions and breaks, and a quick quiz on what you studied.

  • It runs your computer

    Opens programs, plays music, sets reminders and shuts the computer down, all by voice.

  • It learns from you

    Tell it “that is wrong” and it asks what failed and does not repeat it.
